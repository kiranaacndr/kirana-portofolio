04 / Field notes

The conversation
is part of the service.

A closer look at how communication appears in everyday operations at Halla Pilates Studio.

Field note · Halla Pilates Studio

Small messages.
Real experiences.

Role: Receptionist Admin
Focus: Member communication and booking coordination

Context

Members use direct chat to ask about class schedules, make bookings, request changes, and receive reminders. These interactions are part of their experience before they arrive at the studio.

My contribution

I coordinate bookings and schedules, respond to member enquiries, send reminders, and keep attendance information updated. The role requires clear information, careful checking, and a friendly tone.

What I learned

Effective customer communication depends on both the message and the process behind it. Accuracy, responsiveness, and empathy all contribute to a more consistent service experience.

This is a general reflection on my role. No member names, private conversations, or confidential business information are displayed.
